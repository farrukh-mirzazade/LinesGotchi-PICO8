# LinesGotchi TZ

## Product Idea

LinesGotchi is a PICO-8 fantasy-console game that combines Color Lines with a Tamagotchi-like virtual pet.

The game should feel like a compact autonomous pocket toy rather than a large RPG system.

## Core Direction

- Lines is the pet's mini-game.
- Playing Lines raises the pet's happiness.
- Playing Lines makes the pet hungrier.
- Feeding restores fullness but increases weight.
- Playing Lines can reduce weight slightly.
- Long-term progress comes from local records, generations, and discovered species.

## Avoid In MVP

- Online multiplayer.
- Global leaderboard.
- Shop.
- Currency.
- Inventory.
- Skill tree.
- Crafting.
- Complex RPG progression.
