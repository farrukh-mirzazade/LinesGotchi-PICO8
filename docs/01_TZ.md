# LinesGotchi TZ

## Product Idea

LinesGotchi is a PICO-8 fantasy-console game that combines Color Lines with a Tamagotchi-like virtual pet.

The game should feel like a compact autonomous pocket toy rather than a large RPG system.

## Core Direction

- Lines is the pet's mini-game.
- Playing Lines raises the pet's happiness.
- Playing Lines makes the pet hungrier.
- Feeding restores fullness but increases weight.
- Playing Lines can reduce weight slightly.
- Long-term progress comes from local records, generations, and discovered species.

## Current Product Decisions

- The pet uses one classic virtual-pet LCD with seven selectable care icons
  and one non-selectable attention indicator.
- PICO-8 Left/Right act as the original A button, `O` acts as B/confirm, and
  `X` acts as C/cancel.
- Care icons: Food, Light, Game, Medicine, Toilet, Status, Discipline.
- Food offers Meal and Snack. Meal restores fullness; Snack raises happiness;
  both add weight. Food is always available like the original toy.
- Game launches Lines, the LinesGotchi replacement for the original guessing
  mini-game.
- Board size: `8x8`.
- Ball colors: `5`.
- Clear length: `5+`.
- Valid lines: horizontal, vertical, and both diagonals.
- New balls after a non-scoring move: `3`.
- Input: keyboard/gamepad PICO-8 controls.
- Hunger scale: `0 = full`, `100 = starving`.
- UI shows `full = 100 - hunger`, so feeding visibly fills the bar.
- Happiness scale: `0..100`.
- Feeding improves fullness and weight, not happiness.
- Neglect can cause illness and death. `O` starts a new egg after death.
- Growth stage is driven by virtual age and care quality, not RPG levels.

## Avoid In MVP

- Online multiplayer.
- Global leaderboard.
- Shop.
- Currency.
- Inventory.
- Skill tree.
- Crafting.
- Complex RPG progression or food inventory.
