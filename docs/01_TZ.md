# PicoGotchi TZ

## Product Idea

PicoGotchi is a PICO-8 fantasy-console virtual-pet game. Care, lifespan and
evolution are the primary experience. Color Lines remains one optional
mini-game available through the Game icon.

The game should feel like a compact autonomous pocket toy rather than a large RPG system.

## Core Direction

- The pet is the product; mini-games support its needs and development.
- Lines is one optional activity, not the product identity.
- Playing Lines raises the pet's happiness.
- Playing Lines makes the pet hungrier.
- Feeding restores fullness but increases weight.
- Playing Lines can reduce weight slightly.
- Long-term progress comes from generations, discovered original species and
  compact life records.
- Evolution is based on accumulated care quality, not a Lines score or RPG
  level.

## Current Product Decisions

- The pet uses one classic virtual-pet LCD with nine selectable care icons
  and one non-selectable attention indicator, arranged five above and five
  below the pet.
- PICO-8 Left/Right act as the original A button, `O` acts as B/confirm, and
  `X` acts as C/cancel.
- Care icons: Food, Light, Game, Medicine, Toilet, Status, Discipline, Records,
  Sound and Attention.
- Food offers Meal and Snack. Meal restores fullness; Snack raises happiness;
  both add weight. Food is always available like the original toy.
- Game currently launches Lines. It remains the first PicoGotchi mini-game,
  while the architecture must allow additional activities later.
- Board size: `8x8`.
- Ball colors: `5`.
- Clear length: `5+`.
- Valid lines: horizontal, vertical, and both diagonals.
- New balls after a non-scoring move: `3`.
- Input: keyboard/gamepad PICO-8 controls.
- Hunger scale: `0 = full`, `100 = starving`.
- UI shows `full = 100 - hunger`, so feeding visibly fills the bar.
- Happiness scale: `0..100`.
- Feeding improves fullness and weight, not happiness.
- Neglect can cause illness and death. `O` starts a new egg after death.
- Growth stage is driven by virtual age and care quality, not RPG levels.
- Initial target roster: one Egg, one Baby, two Child forms, three Teen forms
  and five Adult forms.
- Normal growth reaches Adult after approximately six real days. An accelerated
  profile exists for testing and shorter sessions.
- Closed time is simulated on launch with a 12-hour cap. Offline processing can
  reduce health to 1 but cannot kill the pet before the player regains control.
- Death permanently ends the current life; history, discoveries and generation
  count survive the new Egg.
- Evolution requirements are hidden from the player.
- Accelerated mode reaches Adult after 60 minutes.
- Each pet receives a player-entered name.
- Name input supports both an on-screen keyboard and direct physical keyboard
  typing. Names are limited to six characters.
- Status pages use heart indicators without exposing exact numeric values.
- Creatures are sexless in the first version; breeding and inherited traits
  are outside the initial scope.
- See [[14_PICOGOTCHI_REFERENCE_ANALYSIS]] for the reference-derived design
  constraints and implementation order.

## Avoid In MVP

- Online multiplayer.
- Global leaderboard.
- Shop.
- Currency.
- Inventory.
- Skill tree.
- Crafting.
- Complex RPG progression or food inventory.
